# Category & Age-Tag Plan

Built from the owner's rough product list plus a scrape of two real Kerala baby
stores — Pretty Plums (prettyplums.com, Kottayam) and Mom N Cute
(momncute.com, Kattappana) — cross-checked against FirstCry's national
skincare taxonomy. Compiled 4 Aug 2026.

## Categories

1. **Diapering** — Diapers (Tape), Diapers (Pant), Wipes, Changing accessories
2. **Bath & Skincare** — Baby wash, Cleansing bar, Shampoo, Lotion, Face
   cream, Massage oil, Protective facial cream, Powder, Diaper rash cream
   (cross-listed with Diapering), Body milk & bubble bath
3. **Feeding** — Feeding pillow, Nipple shields & accessories, Bibs & burp
   cloths
4. **Clothing** — Newborn daily wear, Girls' collection, Boys' collection,
   Thermal/winter wear, Ethnic wear
5. **Nursery & Bedding** — Blankets & swaddles, Carry nest/bedding sets,
   Towels & bath mat
6. **Grooming** — Nail kits, cotton buds, baby kajal
7. **Newborn Kits & Combos** — Hospital bag/starter kits (Pretty Plums'
   signature line — customizable 25–79 item bundles; worth a proper
   build-your-own-kit flow, not just a static combo product)
8. **Toys** — thin at both competitors, low priority for launch
9. **Maternity & Mom Care** — Postpartum body care (stretch mark cream/oil),
   postpartum hair care (anti-hair-fall), nursing & maternity accessories.
   Resolves the "Sebamed Adult / Stretch mark Cream / Anti hair loss" entries
   from the owner's original list — not stray notes, a real category both
   competitors run separately.

Only `Diapering` and `Bath & Skincare` map onto products seeded so far
(existing `cat_diapers` / `cat_skincare` categories cover them). The rest get
created as their product batches arrive.

## Age-stage tag ladder

Multi-select, not a single dropdown — a newborn diaper pack can carry several
rungs at once, matching how Pretty Plums' own filter works. Diapers and
clothing get real use out of the full ladder; skincare mostly doesn't need
finer than "Newborn" vs. "Older."

1. Premature (Mom N Cute runs this as its own category, not just a tag)
2. Newborn (0-2 weeks)
3. 2-4 weeks
4. 1-3 Months
5. 3-6 Months
6. 6-9 Months
7. 9-12 Months
8. 12-18 Months
9. 18-24 Months

Rungs 1, 2, 4 and 5 are the exact literal tags Pretty Plums uses ("New born",
"1-3M", "3-6M"); rung 3 is the owner's own finer-grained addition, useful for
diapers/wipes since consumption changes fastest in the first month.
